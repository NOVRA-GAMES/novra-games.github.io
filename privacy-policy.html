Privacy Policy

Developer: NOVRA · Applies to: Drop & Pop 3D and other NOVRA games · Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information NOVRA ("we", "us") collects when you play our games, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we do not collect

Our games do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. You do not need to create an account to play.

2. Player name, account and cloud save

  • Player name: you can choose a nickname in the game. It is shown to other players (for example on the online leaderboard) and must be unique. Please do not use your real name or personal information as your nickname. Offensive names are blocked or removed.
  • Anonymous account: to save your nickname, leaderboard score and progress online, the game creates an anonymous account ID with Google Firebase Authentication. It is not linked to your real identity.
  • What is stored online: your account ID, nickname, best score, and game progress (levels, coins, gems, unlocked items, settings). It is stored in Google Cloud Firestore.

3. Information collected automatically

To run the game, show ads and improve the experience, some information is collected automatically by third-party services built into the game:

  • Game progress (levels, coins, settings) is stored on your device, and online only backed up to the cloud with your anonymous account described above.
  • Usage and device data such as levels played, game events, app version, device model, operating system, language, approximate country, and an advertising or app-instance identifier.
  • Crash and performance data to help us fix problems.

4. Third-party services

These services may collect and process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data in ads
  • Unity Ads and AppLovin (ad partners that may fill ads through Google AdMob mediation): Unity Ads privacy and applovin.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Authentication and Cloud Firestore for statistics, accounts and cloud save): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing / Unity In-App Purchasing (purchases): payments are handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card or payment details.
  • Unity (game engine services): unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

5. How we use information

  • To run the game and keep a backup of your progress
  • To show your nickname and score on the leaderboard
  • To show ads, including optional rewarded video ads
  • To understand which levels are too hard or easy, and to fix bugs
  • To process in-app purchases through Google Play

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Ads and your choices

Ads may be personalised based on your device's advertising identifier. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and can change their choice at any time. You can also buy "No Ads" in the game to remove forced ads.

7. Children

Our games are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention and deleting your account

Game progress on your device is removed when you uninstall the game. Online data (account ID, nickname, score and cloud save) is kept until you delete it. Analytics data is kept by Google for up to 14 months.

How to delete your account and data:

  • In the game: open Settings and tap "Delete my account & data". Your account, nickname, leaderboard entry and cloud save are deleted immediately.
  • By email: write to the address below with the subject "Delete my data" and your nickname. We will delete your data within 30 days.

9. Security

We rely on the security measures of Google, Unity and Google Play. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
