How to play

Easy to learn, hard to put down. One finger is all you need.

1

Aim

Slide your finger left and right to choose where the next toy falls.

2

Drop

Let go and the toy drops into the glass jar. Watch the bubble to see what comes next.

3

Merge

Two of the same toy touch and pop into one bigger toy: 2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8…

4

Win

Reach the level goal before the toys pile up over the dotted line. Earn up to 3 stars!

Pro tips

  • 🎯 Keep big toys on one side and small toys on the other: merges happen much more often.
  • 👀 Always check the Next bubble before you drop.
  • ⚡ Merge fast: chained merges give a combo bonus.
  • ✨ Every brand-new toy you create gives extra points.
  • 💣 Stuck near the dotted line? Use a Bomb or Fire before it is too late.
  • 🔥 Play every day to keep your streak and grab the daily reward.

FAQ

Is Drop & Pop 3D free?

Yes! The game is free to play. It shows ads, and you can watch optional videos for extra rewards. "No Ads" can be bought in the game to remove forced ads.

When do I lose a level?

If toys stay above the dotted line for too long, the level ends. You can watch a short video to revive and keep going.

How does the leaderboard work?

Every month is a new season. Your score from levels counts for that month. At the end of the month you get a rank badge and gems: Top 10,000 = Gold Popper, Top 1,000 = Diamond, Top 100 = Master, Top 10 = Grand Master and #1 = Pop Legend.

How can I delete my data?

Open Settings in the game and tap "Delete my account & data", or email us. See our Privacy Policy.
